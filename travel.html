How to Get There

For our New Yorkers:

Option 1: Paris → Nîmes

Fly to Paris and take a 3.5-hour high-speed train to Nîmes, the closest major city to the venue. This option is usually slightly cheaper than Option 2.

Flights: French Bee offers low-cost, direct flights from NYC to Paris, though you'll have many airline options.

Trains: High-speed trains (TGV or OuiGo) can be booked on the SNCF website . If you fly into Paris Roissy Charles-de-Gaulle (CDG), you can board a train directly from the airport. If you fly into Orly, take Metro Line 14 to Gare de Lyon.

There are two TGV stations in Nîmes:
• Nîmes Centre (city center)
• Nîmes Pont du Gard (14 km from the city center)

From either station, rent a car (Avis, Alamo, Europcar, etc.). The venue is about a 45-minute drive from Nîmes Centre.

Option 2: Marseille → Venue

Fly to Marseille (usually with a layover in Paris), then rent a car and drive to the venue.

Marseille Airport is about 27 km from the city center. Renting a car directly at the airport is the easiest option.

From Vitrolles or Marseille's main station (Gare St. Charles), you can take a regional TER or Intercités train (not high-speed) to Nîmes and rent a car there.

You can also simply rent a car directly in Marseille, which is likely the easiest overall. The venue is a 2-2.5 hour drive from Marseille. Again, there’s lots to see along the way, see our map below :]
