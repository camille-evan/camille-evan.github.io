Lodging for International Travelers

The wedding will take place at this domaine in the Gard region near the Cévennes mountains.

Airbnbs are readily available in the region and probably the easiest way to find accommodation. There's also Gîtes de France, which is a more local vacation rental website.

Reach out to us if you have any trouble finding good options and we'll help you!

How to Get There

For our New Yorkers:

Option 1: Fly to Paris, train to Nîmes, bus/drive to venue

Fly to Paris, take a 3.5-hour high-speed train to Nîmes, then rent a car and drive to the wedding venue. This is usually slightly cheaper than Option 2.

Flights: French Bee has low-cost, direct flights from NYC to Paris, but you'll have many airline options.

Trains: High-speed trains (TGV or OuiGo) can be booked on the SNCF website. If you fly into Paris Roissy Charles-de-Gaulle airport, you can get on a train directly from the airport (enter CDG as your departing station). If you fly into Orly, you'll need to take the metro 14 to Paris city center for the TGV station there, called Gare de Lyon.

Note that there are two TGV stations in Nîmes:
• Nîmes Centre (city center)
• Nîmes Pont du Gard (14 km from the city center)

From either station, you can take the bus, a taxi, or rent a car. Obviously I recommend the bus, the 140 runs every 70 mins or so from Nîmes and costs €2. Yep! Amen. Your stop is Sauve - Jeu du Mail, which is a nearby town so we'll have to come pick you up but that's no big deal.

Uber does operate in Nîmes, which will cost roughly €100 or $115 USD. So will Bolt, which is another rideshare company that may be a little more popular in the region so maybe easier to find a car. There's also G7 which is a traditional taxi service that could be better for larger groups (5-7 passengers).

You can rent a car using Avis, Alamo, Europcar, etc. Recommended if you're staying longer in the region and want to explore more. The venue is about a 45-minute drive from Nîmes city center. There's lots to see in Nimes and along the way, check out our map!

Option 2: Fly to Marseille, drive to venue or train to Nîmes

Fly to Marseille (usually with a layover in Lisbon or Paris), then rent a car and drive to the venue.

Aéroport Marseille-Provence is about 27 km from the city center. The easiest option (if traveling directly to the venue) is to rent a car at the airport, but you can also take transit to either the city center or the nearby Vitrolles train station.

From Vitrolles or Marseille's main station (Gare St. Charles), you can take a regional TER or Intercités train (not high-speed) to Nîmes and take a bus or rent a car from there.

The venue is a 2-2.5 hour drive from Marseille. Again, there's lots to see along the way, see our map below :)
