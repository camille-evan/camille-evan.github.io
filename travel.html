How to Get There

For our New Yorkers:

Option 1: Paris → Nîmes

Fly to Paris and take a 3.5-hour high-speed train to Nîmes, the closest major city to the wedding venue. This option is usually slightly cheaper than Option 2.

Flights: French Bee offers low-cost, direct flights from NYC to Paris, but you’ll have many airline options.

Trains: High-speed trains (TGV or OuiGo) can be booked on the SNCF website. If you fly into Paris Charles-de-Gaulle (CDG), you can board the train directly from the airport. If you arrive at Orly, take Metro Line 14 into the city to Gare de Lyon.

Note that there are two stations in Nîmes:
• Nîmes Centre (city center)
• Nîmes Pont du Gard (14 km outside the city)

From either station, rent a car (Avis, Alamo, Europcar, etc.). The venue is about a 45-minute drive from Nîmes Centre. There’s lots to see in Nîmes and along the way — check out our map.

Option 2: Marseille → Venue

Fly to Marseille (usually with a layover in Paris), then rent a car and drive to the venue.

Marseille Airport is about 27 km from the city center. Renting a car directly at the airport is the easiest option.

You can also take transit to Marseille city center or to Vitrolles train station, then take a regional TER or Intercités train (not high-speed) to Nîmes and rent a car there.

Driving directly from Marseille is often the simplest overall option. The venue is a 2–2.5 hour drive, with plenty to see along the way — see our map 🙂
